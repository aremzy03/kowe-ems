What we heard

The brief

A multi-tenant school management system for Nigerian K-12 schools: records, results, fees and attendance, with an AI layer. The name comes from the Yoruba Kọ́ wé, to write and record. The mark should feel credible to proprietors and parents, friendly to teachers, and clearly Nigerian without leaning on cliché.

crediblefriendlyrootedintelligent

Success: reads at 16 px, works in one colour and reversed, carries the Yoruba ọ as an ownable detail, and sits naturally on the Paper Brutalism palette.
