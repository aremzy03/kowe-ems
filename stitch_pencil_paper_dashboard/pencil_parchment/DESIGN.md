## Brand & Style

This design system translates the intimate, focused craft of analog drafting and journaling into a digital interface. It rejects frictionless, sterile digital surfaces in favor of tactile skeuomorphism, editorial restraint, and organic craftsmanship. The emotional atmosphere is meditative, deliberate, and archival—evoking warm heavyweight cotton paper, mechanical pencils, and field notebooks.

### Target Audience & Feel
Built for writers, architects, designers, essayists, and tactile thinkers who value physical connection, permanence, and mindful work over disposable micro-interactions. The interface should feel collected rather than assembled: quiet, weighted, and warm to the touch.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Materiality:** Cream sketch surfaces with paper fiber tones, layered cardstock depth, and subtle deckle-edge softness.
- **Mark-Making:** Strict avoidance of glossy neon states or plastic radiuses. Accents behave like dry colored pencil pigment, lithographic crayon, or dry graphite stamps.
- **Structural Atmosphere:** Faint ruled guide-lines, architectural grid patterns, margin ticks, and notebook tab indexes that frame content like an open studio sketchbook.

## Colors

The palette derives strictly from dry drawing media and archival paper stocks.

### Base Surfaces
- **Canvas / Ivory:** `#FBF9F5` — Primary viewport canvas; bright cream vellum.
- **Page Surface / Cream:** `#F4EFE6` — Elevated sheet, note cards, and main containers.
- **Dossier / Kraft:** `#EAE3D2` — Recessed wells, backings, sticky side panels, and folder tabs.
- **Faint Guideline / Rule:** `#D8D2C5` — Ledger lines, dotted drafting grids, and separator rules.

### Graphite Core (Monochrome Hierarchy)
- **Deep 2B Graphite (Primary):** `#2B2B2A` — Primary titles, active stroke lines, key CTAs, and structural borders.
- **Soft HB Graphite:** `#4A4A48` — Body prose, primary icons, and secondary buttons.
- **Light Sketch Gray:** `#858582` — Captions, timestamps, disabled markers, and placeholder text.

### Colored Pencil Accents
- **Terracotta Prismacolor (Secondary):** `#C86D51` — Critical interactions, selections, editorial annotations, and primary highlights.
- **Muted Sage Chalk (Tertiary):** `#6C826B` — Success states, secondary tags, verified stamps, and organic categories.
- **Washed Indigo Lead:** `#536B82` — Links, archival indexes, informational callouts, and secondary indicators.
- **Pencil Erase / Canvas White:** `#FFFDF9` — Pure unworked paper highlights and active text fields.

## Typography

The type system balances classic editorial book composition with mechanical drafting notation.

### Font Roles
- **Editorial Voice (`Newsreader`):** Used for headlines and running text. Featuring optical sizes and nuanced serifs, it establishes an academic, literary atmosphere. Italics should be used generously for emphasis, quotes, and authorial notes.
- **Drafting & Indexing (`Courier Prime`):** Used for metadata, labels, tags, tabs, and technical measurements. It introduces the mechanical rhythm of a manual typewriter and field instrument annotations.

### Typesetting Rules
- Maintain proportional baseline rhythm: multi-line headings must breathe with roomy line-heights.
- Monospaced labels should use all-caps for categories, filing tags, and status stamps.
- Avoid tight letter-spacing on serif text; preserve natural book-press tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts reference opened stationery, architect folios, and lined notebook spreads.

### Grid Philosophy
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Content sits on an editorial grid framed within wide parchment margins (`margin: 2.5rem`). Multi-column articles and split workbench views mimic adjacent open sketchbook pages.
- **Mobile (Single/Split Column):** Collapses into a single notepad block with tight, ruled margins (`margin: 1.25rem`).

### Rhythm & Alignment
- **Lined Notebook Geometry:** Spacing tokens snap to multiples of `0.25rem` (4px). Major editorial sections should align to an intentional 24px vertical rhythm reminiscent of standard ruled stationery.
- **Page Edges:** Containers do not cling to viewport bounds; they float within safe parchment margins to reinforce the sensation of a physical sheet resting on an artist’s desk.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is modeled after stacked physical sheets of paper, cardboard mats, and graphite pressure—never digital glows or multi-axis colored lighting.

### Shadow Architecture
- **Flat Paper (Level 0):** Flush against background; borders defined by `#D8D2C5` or single `#2B2B2A` outlines.
- **Resting Sheet (Level 1 - Cards, Notebook Spreads):**
  - Offset shadow: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(43, 43, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 43, 42, 0.08)`
  - Recreates the soft cast shadow of 120gsm bond paper sitting on drafting wood.
- **Lifted Folio / Floating Sheet (Level 2 - Modals, Popovers, Tabs):**
  - Offset shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 43, 42, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 43, 42, 0.06)`
  - Simulates a page held up or elevated by a clip.

### Tactile Depth Details
- **Graphite Press (Pressed States):** Negative depth achieved with an inset shadow (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(43, 43, 42, 0.2)`), replicating pencil lead pressed deep into soft paper pulp.
- **Ruled Background Underlays:** Surface layers can feature an optional CSS linear repeating grid or ruled guide line (`1px solid #D8D2C5` spaced at 24px intervals) behind content wells.

## Shapes

The design system favors crisp, slightly softened organic cuts over aggressive rounded pills or clinical machine-squared corners.

### Geometry & Edge Treatments
- **Standard Corners (`roundedness: 1`):** A subtle `0.25rem` (4px) curve mimics paper guillotine trimming, removing harsh digital vectors while preserving an architectural profile.
- **Cardstock Edges (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem`):** Used for cards, note slips, and floating modals.
- **Irregular & Asymmetric Borders:** Where supported by CSS/SVG masks, key accent sheets and sticky notes should use subtle asymmetric border radii (e.g., `255px 15px 225px 15px / 15px 225px 15px 255px` or `4px 2px 3px 5px`) to evoke genuine hand-cut paper and freehand pencil outlines.
- **Filing Tabs:** Top corners rounded at `0.375rem`, bottom corners left flush (`0px`) against the binder seam.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Graphite Stamp):** Solid `#2B2B2A` fill with `#FBF9F5` typography in `Courier Prime` label style. Pressed state exhibits an inset shadow and a slight scale down (`0.99`). Border: 1.5px solid `#2B2B2A`.
- **Secondary (Pencil Outline):** Background `#F4EFE6`, border 1.5px solid `#2B2B2A` (with optional hand-drawn border effect), text in `#2B2B2A`. Hover transitions fill to `#EAE3D2`.
- **Colored Pencil Action:** Background `#C86D51` or `#6C826B` with white paper text, styled as if shaded firmly with colored pencil wax.

### Cards & Paper Sheets
- Background in `#F4EFE6` or `#FBF9F5`. Border is 1px solid `#D8D2C5`.
- Header separator utilizes a faint ruled line or perforated dashed border (`border-bottom: 1.5px dashed #D8D2C5`).
- Elevation adheres to Level 1 resting sheet shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- Modeled after index ledger stamps or archival tape tabs.
- Typography: `label-sm` in `Courier Prime`, uppercase.
- Outline style: 1px solid `#858582`, background `#EAE3D2`, padding `2px 8px`.
- Color coded with dry colored pencil undertones: Terracotta (`#C86D51`), Sage (`#6C826B`), Indigo (`#536B82`).

### Form Inputs
- **Text Fields:** Background `#FFFDF9`, bottom border only (1.5px solid `#4A4A48`) to resemble writing on a ledger sheet, or full enclosure with 1px border `#D8D2C5`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to 1.5px solid `#2B2B2A` with an imperceptible terracotta hint (`outline: 1px solid #C86D51`).
- **Placeholder Text:** Rendered in `#858582` using italicized `Newsreader` or faint `Courier Prime`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox:** Square box with 1.5px `#2B2B2A` border. The checked state is a distinct hand-drawn diagonal hatch or heavy graphite "X" / check stroke, avoiding standard vector symbols.
- **Radio Button:** Circular outline with a hand-filled graphite core dot when selected.

### Lists & Ledger Tables
- Rows demarcated by horizontal notebook ruled lines (`1px solid #D8D2C5`).
- Left-hand margins can feature an optional faint red/terracotta vertical margin rule (`1.5px solid rgba(200, 109, 81, 0.4)`), mimicking ruled writing stationery.

### Specialized Components
- **Index Tabs:** Folder tabs protruding from card or panel edges, styled with `#EAE3D2`, labeled in vertical or horizontal `Courier Prime`, establishing workspace hierarchy.
- **Pencil Annotation / Callout:** Background `#FBF9F5`, left border 3px solid `#C86D51`, body set in italic `Newsreader` to read as editorial marginalia.
